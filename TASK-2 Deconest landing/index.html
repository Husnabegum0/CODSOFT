Our Story

Where Vision Meets Masterful Craftsmanship

Deconest Interior Studio is Bengaluru's premier luxury interior design firm, dedicated to creating spaces that are as beautiful as they are livable.

Founded with a passion for transforming ordinary spaces into extraordinary living experiences, we bring together a curated team of visionary designers, skilled craftsmen, and project specialists who share a singular commitment to excellence.

Every project begins with listening — understanding your lifestyle, aspirations, and the story you want your space to tell. The result is always a home that feels uniquely, unmistakably yours.
